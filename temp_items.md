terms:
- HBTU: hit by this unit - when a unit with this item uses an attack on another unit, this item should trigger its effect. the duration of this effect should be 1 second at base, but is flexible
- ATU: attacking this unit - when an enemy unit uses its attack on a unit with this item, this item should trigger it's effect. the duration of this effect should be 1 second at base, but is flexible
- passive: this item should always give its effect. this should usually, but not always, be a bonus to the user's stats 

Items to create:

buff: 
- passive: increased attack speed (flat number)
- passive: increased attack range (percentage)
- passive: increased mana generated when hitting an enemy (flat number)
- passive: increased mana generated hit by an enemy (flat number)
- passive: increased armor (both flat number and percentage, percentage rarer)
- passive: increased magic resistance (both flat number and percentage, percentage rarer)
- passive: units within a certain amount of tiles gain increased stats (1 item per stat, apply above passives, at lower values than actually having the item)
- passive: bonus damage dealt on hit (item has a cooldown independent from attack speed, rare)

debuff:
- HBTU: attack speed decreased
- ATU: attack speed decreased
- ATU: decreased mana gain
- HBTU: magic resistance decreased
- HBTU: armor decreased
- HBTU: attack damage decreased
- ATU: attack damage decreased
- ATU: armor decreased
- ATU: magic resistance decreased
- ATU: ability power decreased
- HBTU: ability power decreased
- ATU: portion of damage dealt is reflected (item has a cooldown, rare)
